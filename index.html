Why I built Kimosabe Tools

I noticed that most "free" PDF tools online actually take your files and upload them to their servers. I wanted to create a space where you can get your work done without ever worrying about your privacy or data security.

Kimosabe Tools is a solo project built on a simple promise: Your files never leave your device. If these tools save you time, a small donation helps me keep the servers running and the tools ad-free.
