Frequently Asked Questions

How do I crop an image?

Click Crop, then drag on the image to draw a crop area, resize it with the corner handles, and click Apply Crop. You can also pick an aspect ratio like 1:1 or 16:9.

Can I rotate and flip as well?

Yes. Rotate the image left or right in 90-degree steps, and flip it horizontally or vertically, before or after cropping.

What formats can I download?

You can download your edited image as a JPG or a PNG. PNG preserves transparency.

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. All editing happens inside your browser using the Canvas API. Your image never leaves your device.
