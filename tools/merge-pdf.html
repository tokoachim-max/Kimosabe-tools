    Frequently Asked Questions

    How many PDF files can I merge at once?

    Up to 30 PDF files, with a maximum combined size of 200 MB.

    Can I reorder the PDFs before merging?

    Yes. After uploading, use the up and down arrows to change the order before clicking Merge.

    Will my PDFs be uploaded to a server?

    No. Merging happens entirely in your browser. Your documents are never sent to any server.

    Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

    No. Password-protected PDFs cannot be processed. Remove the password from each file first, then merge them.
