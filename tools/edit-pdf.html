Frequently Asked Questions

Can I fill in a PDF form?

Yes. Use the Text tool to click on any form field and type your answer.

Will added text be searchable?

No. The editor flattens all annotations as images in the PDF.

Can I undo my changes?

Yes. Ctrl+Z to undo, Ctrl+Y to redo. Up to 50 steps stored per session.

Can I paste images from clipboard?

Yes. Copy any image and press Ctrl+V to paste it directly onto the page.
