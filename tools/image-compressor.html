Frequently Asked Questions

Does image compression reduce quality?

It depends on the quality level you choose. At 80% quality most images look identical to the original but are 50–70% smaller. Use the live preview slider to find the right balance before downloading.

Can I compress PNG images?

Yes. For PNG files you can either keep them as PNG (lossless) or convert them to JPEG or WebP for much smaller file sizes.

Is there a file size limit?

Yes, 100MB per image. Most images are well under this limit.

Why is my compressed image the same size?

If your image is already highly optimised, re-encoding it may not reduce the size further. Our tool automatically returns the original file if compression would make it larger.
