Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reorder pages?

Drag any page thumbnail to a new spot, or use the left and right arrow buttons on each page. The new order is used when you save.

Can I delete or extract only some pages?

Yes. Select the pages you want, then either delete them or choose "Keep selected only" to extract just those pages into a new PDF.

Can I rotate individual pages?

Yes. Each page thumbnail has a rotate button, and the rotation is baked into the saved PDF.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Pages are rendered and rebuilt inside your browser using JavaScript. Your file never leaves your device.
