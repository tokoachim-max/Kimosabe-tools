Frequently Asked Questions

Is an electronic signature drawn in a browser legally binding?

An image-based drawn signature is not a cryptographically certified signature. It provides a visual representation but does not include a digital certificate or audit trail. For legally binding e-signatures, use a certified service like DocuSign.

Can I drag my signature to an exact position?

Yes. After creating your signature, the live preview shows your actual PDF page. Drag the signature overlay to place it exactly where you need it.

What signature input methods are supported?

Three methods: draw freehand with mouse, finger, or stylus; type your name and choose a cursive font; or upload an existing signature image (PNG with transparency works best).

Can I add my signature to every page?

Yes. Select "All Pages" in the position options to embed your signature on every page of the document.
