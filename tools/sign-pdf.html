Frequently Asked Questions

Is an electronic signature drawn in a browser legally binding?

An image-based drawn signature is not a cryptographically certified signature. It provides a visual representation of a signature but does not include a digital certificate or audit trail. For legally binding e-signatures, use a certified service like DocuSign.

Can I choose which page my signature appears on?

Yes. After uploading, use the page selector to choose any page. It defaults to the last page, which is standard for most contracts.

What colours can I sign in?

Black, blue, red, green, and purple.

Can I add my signature to every page?

Yes. Select 'All Pages' in the position options to embed your signature on every page of the document.
