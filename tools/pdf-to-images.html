Frequently Asked Questions

What image formats can I export to?

You can export each PDF page as a JPG or a PNG. JPG is smaller and best for photos; PNG is lossless and best for text or line art.

Can I download all pages at once?

Yes. Once conversion finishes you can download every page in a single ZIP file, or save individual pages one at a time.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Every page is rendered inside your browser using JavaScript. Your file never leaves your device.

Can I control the image resolution?

Yes. A resolution slider lets you render pages at higher DPI for sharper images, or lower DPI for smaller files.
