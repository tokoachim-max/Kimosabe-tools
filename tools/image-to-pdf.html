    Frequently Asked Questions

    How many images can I convert to one PDF?

    Up to 30 images per conversion, with a combined limit of 50 MB total.

    What page sizes are supported?

    A4, Letter, Legal, and Fit to Image (page sized exactly to each image).

    Will the PDF have good quality?

    Yes. Images are embedded at their original resolution with no re-encoding, so quality is preserved exactly.

    Can I reorder images before converting?

    Yes. After uploading, drag images up or down in the list to set the page order before converting.
