Frequently Asked Questions

What image formats can I convert between?

You can convert between JPG, PNG, and WebP. GIF is not supported for output as browsers cannot encode GIF files natively.

Will converting reduce image quality?

Converting PNG to PNG or WebP to WebP keeps full quality. Converting to JPEG uses the quality slider — 85% is a good balance between quality and file size.

Does converting resize my image?

No. The converter preserves your original resolution exactly. Use our Resize Image tool if you need to change dimensions.

Why does my PNG look different after converting to JPEG?

JPEG does not support transparency. Transparent areas are filled with white when converting to JPEG.
