Frequently Asked Questions

Where can the page numbers be placed?

Anywhere along the top or bottom of the page — left, centre, or right — using the nine-position picker.

Can I start numbering from a specific number?

Yes. Set any starting number, so you can begin at 1, skip a cover page, or continue numbering from another document.

What number formats are available?

Plain numbers (1, 2, 3), Page X, and Page X of Y are all supported.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Page numbers are added inside your browser using JavaScript. Your file never leaves your device.
