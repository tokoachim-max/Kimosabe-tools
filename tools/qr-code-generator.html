Frequently Asked Questions

What can I put in a QR code?

A website link, plain text, Wi-Fi network details, an email address, or a phone number. The tool builds the correct encoding for each type.

Can I download the QR code as an SVG?

Yes. You can download a high-resolution PNG or a scalable SVG that stays sharp at any print size.

Do the QR codes expire?

No. These are static QR codes — the data is encoded directly in the image, so they never expire and do not rely on our servers.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. QR codes are generated entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Whatever you encode never leaves your device.
